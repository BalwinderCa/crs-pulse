# Privacy Policy — CRS Pulse

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## 1. Introduction

CRS Pulse ("we", "our", "us") operates the CRS Pulse mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our app.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address
- Name
- Password (stored as a cryptographic hash — we never store your plain text password)
- Google account identifier (if using Google Sign-In)

### Profile Information
- CRS (Comprehensive Ranking System) score
- Express Entry category selection
- Notification preferences

### Device Information
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications
- Device platform (Android/iOS)
- App version

### Usage Data
- Draw history views
- Analytics interactions (category and period filters selected)
- Notification read/unread status

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and personalize the CRS Pulse service
- Send push notifications about new Express Entry draws (when enabled)
- Send weekly summary notifications (when enabled)
- Generate personalized CRS score predictions
- Improve our services and fix technical issues

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with:
- **Firebase (Google):** For push notification delivery
- **Hosting providers:** For secure API and database hosting
- **Legal authorities:** When required by law

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmission
- Bcrypt password hashing
- Sanctum token-based authentication with expiry
- Rate limiting to prevent abuse
- Input validation and sanitization

## 6. Data Retention

- Account data: Retained while your account is active
- Deleted accounts: All data removed within 30 days
- Notification history: Retained for 90 days
- FCM tokens: Removed on logout or token refresh

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction:** Update inaccurate information via the Profile screen
- **Deletion:** Delete your account from the Profile screen (Settings → Delete Account)
- **Opt-out:** Disable push notifications at any time in Settings

## 8. Children's Privacy

CRS Pulse is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Third-Party Services

- **Google Firebase:** Push notifications and authentication — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google Sign-In:** Optional authentication — governed by Google's privacy policy

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of material changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests:
- Email: privacy@crspulse.ca
- Response time: Within 30 days
