# CRS Pulse Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 18, 2026  
**App:** CRS Pulse – Express Entry Calculator  
**Contact:** contact@crspulse.com

## Summary

CRS Pulse is a **free**, local-first mobile app supported by banner ads (served by Google AdMob). There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Your CRS profile data stays on your device, and we do not operate user accounts. The only data we send to **our own** servers is an anonymous push token, if you enable draw alerts, and an anonymous outline of your timeline, if you choose to share it in Community. The app also sends anonymous usage analytics (which screens and features are used) to PostHog, which you can turn off in Settings. We never collect your CRS profile off-device. The website, crspulse.com, may show ads served by Google AdSense.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores locally (via AsyncStorage):

- CRS calculator inputs (age, education, language scores, work experience)
- Calculated CRS score and program category
- Theme and notification preferences
- Cached IRCC draw history
- Immigration timeline milestones (if you use the timeline feature)
- Community sharing choices (display name, avatar, program) and the timelines you have blocked

This data is not transmitted to our servers, except the outline of your timeline if you share it in Community (see below).

## iOS App Store privacy labels

When completing Apple's App Privacy questionnaire:

- CRS calculator inputs are stored on device only and are not collected. Timeline data is not collected unless you share it in Community.
- **User Content → Other User Content:** a Community timeline you choose to share (milestone types, day counts, optional months, AOR month), used for app functionality, not linked to identity, not used for tracking.
- **Identifiers → User ID:** the display name you choose for Community (optional) and a random key our server issues when you share, used for app functionality (to show, update and delete your shared timeline), not linked to identity, not used for tracking.
- **Usage Data → Product Interaction:** anonymous screen views and taps (PostHog), used for analytics, not linked to identity, not used for tracking.
- **Location → Coarse Location:** approximate location derived from the IP address (PostHog), used for analytics, not linked to identity, not used for tracking.
- **Identifiers → Device ID:** a random analytics ID generated by the app (PostHog), not linked to identity. Also an anonymous push notification token (only if you enable draw alerts), and a device advertising identifier used by Google AdMob for advertising and measurement, only with your App Tracking Transparency consent.

## Data sent to third parties

### IRCC (Government of Canada)

Draw history is fetched directly from the official public JSON feed at `canada.ca`. No personal data is sent — only a standard HTTP request.

### Push notifications (optional)

If you enable draw notifications, the app registers an anonymous Expo push token with our Cloudflare Worker so we can alert you when IRCC publishes a new draw. No CRS profile data is included — only the device token and platform (iOS/Android).

You can disable notifications and revoke your token at any time in Settings.

### Community timelines (optional)

Community lets you share an outline of your immigration timeline with other app users and see theirs. Nothing is shared unless you tap **Share with community**.

- **What is shared:** the types of milestones you've reached (for example "AOR Received"), how many days after your first milestone each one happened, the month of your AOR, your program (for example CEC), and, only if you turn it on, the month and year of each milestone. Also the display name and preset avatar you choose; both are optional.
- **What is never shared:** exact dates, notes, custom milestones, milestones dated in the future, your CRS profile or score, and anything that identifies you. Avatars are drawn in the app from a preset list; no photo is uploaded.
- **Who can see it:** anyone using the app's Community feature. Display names are checked against a list of offensive words, links, contact details and names that impersonate IRCC or the app.
- **Where it's stored:** our Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare D1 database, with a random key our server issues to your device. Only a one-way hash of that key is stored with your timeline. Your device's IP address is used briefly to limit abuse and is not stored with your timeline.
- **Updates:** while you share, changes to your timeline are sent automatically, so your shared outline stays current.
- **Reports and blocking:** you can report a timeline (spam, offensive name, fake or misleading, other) or block it so you no longer see it; blocking is stored on your device only. A report stores the reason and a one-way hash of the reporter's key, or of their IP address if they have never shared. A timeline reported by three different people is hidden automatically, and we review reports and remove content that breaks these rules, normally within 24 hours.
- **Stopping:** **Stop sharing**, or **Reset All Data** in Settings, deletes your shared timeline and any reports about it from our servers immediately. Removing every milestone from your timeline does the same.

### App updates (Expo)

From version 1.0.14, the app checks Expo's update service (`u.expo.dev`) when it starts, so we can deliver fixes without waiting for a store release. The request includes the app version, platform and a random ID for the installation that Expo uses to count devices. It never includes your calculator inputs, CRS score, timeline or any other profile data. Like any internet request, it reveals your device's IP address to Expo.

### PostHog (usage analytics, optional)

The app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog so we can see which features are used and whether people come back. Each event carries a random ID generated on first launch, the screen name, the name of the tapped component (never its text), app version, device model, OS, locale and time zone. It never includes your calculator inputs, CRS score, timeline, checklist or any other profile data, and it is not linked to your identity. There is no session recording.

Like any internet request, sending these events reveals your device's IP address to PostHog. PostHog currently stores that IP address with each event and uses it to estimate an approximate location (country, region and city — not your precise location). We use this only to see which countries and regions the app is used in. We do not use it to identify you or combine it with any other data, and we do not share it with anyone. Newer versions of the app send these events through `t.crspulse.com`, an address on our domain that is operated by PostHog; the data still goes to PostHog and is handled the same way.

Analytics is disabled in development builds.

### Website (crspulse.com)

The website uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. For each visit it records the page, the referring site, your country, browser, operating system and device type. Vercel does not store your IP address or build a profile of you, and visits cannot be linked across days or across websites. The website also sends page views, time on page and clicks to PostHog, through `t.crspulse.com`. Click events record which link or button was clicked, never the text on the page, so calculator results are not included. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device: PostHog recognises a returning visitor only within the same day, from a one-way hash it computes on its servers. As with the app, PostHog stores the IP address and an approximate location (country, region and city) with each event. There is no session recording.

The calculators on the website run entirely in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

The analytics above set no cookies. Ads on the website (below) are the exception.

### Google AdSense (website ads)

The website may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads and measure them, which can include ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve those ads. Ad requests never include anything you type into the calculators.

You can turn off personalised advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or opt out of many third-party vendors at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices). Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalised. [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) explains this in more detail.

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). On iOS, the app first asks for App Tracking Transparency permission; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your device identifier.

## What we do not collect

- No user accounts or login
- No in-app purchases or payment information
- No third-party analytics or trackers other than the anonymous PostHog usage analytics and the AdMob ads SDK in the app, and on the website cookieless page-view analytics (Vercel and PostHog) and Google AdSense ads, all described above
- No session recordings
- No sale of personal data
- No immigration profile data on our servers, other than a Community timeline outline you choose to share

## Data retention

Local data persists until you uninstall the app or use Reset in Settings. Push tokens are removed when you disable notifications or revoke via the worker API. Anonymous analytics events, including the IP address and approximate location stored with them, are retained by PostHog under its standard retention. A shared Community timeline, and any reports about it, are kept until you stop sharing or reset the app. We do not retain any other off-device data.

## Children's privacy

CRS Pulse is not directed at children under 13.
